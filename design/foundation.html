Palette

Dawn is indigo ink on undyed paper; Dusk is the indigo vat. The ink of one is the ground of the other. The accent is the only saturated colour on a surface, so it always marks where you are or what to do next. Semantic colours (good, warn, danger, info) are a separate set and never borrow the accent.

        For sign-off

        What this spec commits the rebuild to. Each line is a decision you can reject on its own.

        Palette

        Beni and ai: indigo neutrals, one safflower accent, Dusk as the indigo vat. Semantic colours are separate, and danger is vermilion, not the accent. Dawn and Dusk text roles reach AA on every surface (table above). Faint text is 3:1 and only for placeholders and disabled states.

        Type

        Bricolage Grotesque for headings; Atkinson Hyperlegible Next and Mono for text and data; the system mincho for the kanji marks. The families load from Google Fonts. index.html gains the link in M4 once you approve.

        The thread

        One mark for "you are here": a 2px safflower line on the current rail item, spine leaf and selected tab. Nothing else uses it.

        Shape and space

        Radii 2 / 4 / 6 / 10, a pill only for counts. The spacing ramp 0–80 is the only source of lengths; hit areas are 32, or 44 on touch.

        Tiers

        1240 folds the inspector under the content, 1080 compacts the rail to marks, 860 turns the spine into a drawer, 700 moves the rail to a bottom tab bar, and 560 drops to one column.

        Not yet

        The component and layout CSS here is a proposal: M4 (shell) and M5 (primitives) write it into the app. Shop themes stay on the default until M13; their ids already re-resolve through the scoped [data-theme] blocks.
