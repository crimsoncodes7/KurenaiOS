Mind is roadmap 5.9, docs/mind-plan.md revision 2, approved 24 October 2026 (decisions A–J, invariants 137–141). It is on branch `feat/mind-plan`. Design prompt 16 (frames 32a onwards) is written; nothing is built yet.

Key choices made by the user:
- the name is "Mind" everywhere, with Psychology and Philosophy as headers inside it;
- 15 minutes a day, ADDED to the other spaces and done on a phone on the bus, so the phone is first-class here (unlike Creatives);
- ONE written prompt per lesson: an explain-back or a position, chosen by the lesson's kind, with bullets welcome;
- authored Socratic trees in the VN scene UI by default, with Ask Kurenai on request;
- the goal is curiosity, thinking and feeding their own VN/story writing, not a university degree.

**Why:** the user wants low friction and story-writing links.

**How to apply:** keep sittings short and phone-friendly, and never ask for two writing tasks in one lesson. Related: [[japanese-study-space]], [[user-made-assets]].
