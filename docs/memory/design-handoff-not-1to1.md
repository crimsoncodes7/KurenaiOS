Do not reproduce a Claude Design handoff 1:1. It does not see the whole app and is out of date in places. When a frame conflicts with how the app already does it, follow the app.

**Why:** 2026-10-30, on the first Creatives desk: the user rejected the design's emblem (every desk has a mastery ring), its taglines ("you against you only", lesson counts, encouragement copy) and its extra cards (Firsts, Suggested next, stage chips) as clutter. "Show no more information than what Code does in its desk." Also asked: the side column beside a study-space hero (daily card + Continue) must never be taller than the hero; on Code, Japanese, Mind and Creatives it is size-contained in study.css and its cards are kept short.

**How to apply:** build a new desk or page on the nearest existing one (Code's desk: ring, name, board, bars, path row; daily card + Continue; analytics + one side card; unit cards). Cut explanatory sentences and sub-lines to the minimum; no dead space; check alignment at 1440, 1180 and phone before calling it done. Related: [[no-dead-space-layout]].
