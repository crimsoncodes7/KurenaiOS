Before you start: the three accounts

Governor → Status → the Data switch beside Preview. Switching reloads the app; an amber chip in the header names a review account and takes you back to Mine.

Mine

Your real account. Nothing here changes unless you act on a real record.

A separate first run. It still shows the weekly plan and the calendar's two "SAMPLE — … (edit me)" starters.

A separate lived-in account: 26 weeks of history, every domain filled, 31 titles, every lab unlocked.
