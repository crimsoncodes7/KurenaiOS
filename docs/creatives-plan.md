# Creatives — a study space for art, design, editing and writing (plan, revision 1)

Roadmap 5.7 item 3, asked for on 20 October 2026 and planned on 22 October
2026, the day the iPad Air arrived. It follows the Code plan's shape: a
plan first, then Claude Design, then a platform release and one track at a
time.

Short answer:
- **One subject, Creatives, with four tracks:** Art, Graphic design,
  Editing and Writing. It is a non-exam subject with its own hue, desk and
  lessons on the shared topic page, like Code and Japanese.
- **The method is deliberate practice, not watching tutorials.** Every
  lesson ends in something made: a drill, a study, an exercise or a piece.
  The app keeps the evidence (the work itself, the time and the
  self-critique) and schedules the theory on FSRS.
- **The iPad is where most of it happens:**
  - drills and studies in the app's own sketchbook, which is the ink
    engine from [ipad-plan.md](ipad-plan.md) §6.8;
  - finished pieces in the apps built for them (Procreate, Clip Studio,
    Affinity, DaVinci Resolve or LumaFusion), brought back as evidence.
- **It is pointed at what you already love:**
  - character art and fan art of what is in your Collection;
  - manga pages and panel flow;
  - VN and light-novel writing;
  - AMVs and edits;
  - covers, logos and UI.
- **Kurenai never makes the work.** AI is a critic you ask, never an
  artist, and it sees only what you choose to send.

The questions in §12 decide the curriculum's starting points. The rest is
proposed and needs your review.

---

## 1. Why this is not "Code with pictures"

Code proves skill by running tests (invariant 107). A drawing has no test
suite, so Creatives needs its own idea of evidence:

| Code | Creatives |
|---|---|
| A task passes its cases | A drill or study is done to its brief, timed, and saved with a self-critique against its rubric |
| Retrieval cards from runnable variations | Theory cards (colour, composition, type, craft terms) on FSRS, plus "redo it" items: the same exercise again weeks later, side by side with the old one |
| A capstone with acceptance cases | A **piece**: a brief, milestones (thumbnails, sketch, refine, final) and a rubric with no automatic row, all self-marked. It is never locked. |
| The Rider validator | The **portfolio import**: an image, a time-lapse, a video link or a document, with its time and tools |

Progress you can see matters most here. The **redo** (§5.3) puts this
month's attempt beside the one from three months ago, and that is the
strongest motivation the space has.

## 2. The four tracks

Each track is a **core path** of units (fundamentals, in order), plus
**projects** aimed at your media. The sizes are honest estimates at 30–45
minutes a day.

### 2.1 Art (the iPad's track)

**Core path:**
- line and mark-making;
- shape and form (boxes, cylinders, constructing objects);
- perspective (one, two and three point);
- value and light;
- colour (hue, value and saturation, harmony, colour temperature, light
  colour);
- gesture and figure (proportions, the mannequin, poses);
- heads and faces (including the anime face's simplifications and why
  they work);
- hands and feet;
- clothing and folds;
- composition;
- environments and backgrounds;
- digital workflow (layers, brushes, clipping, colouring a line drawing).

**Projects:**
- a character sheet of an original or Collection character (turnaround,
  expressions);
- a fan-art illustration;
- a cover in the style of a light novel;
- a four-page manga short (panel flow, reading order, screentone);
- a VN sprite with expression variants (it can feed Japanese's cast).

**Drills (in the sketchbook):**
- timed gesture (30 s, 1, 2 and 5 minutes) from references;
- 100 boxes and ellipses;
- value scales;
- master copies;
- colour swatching from a frame.

### 2.2 Graphic design

**Core path:**
- the principles (contrast, repetition, alignment, proximity, hierarchy);
- typography (anatomy, classification, pairing, kerning, leading, the
  measure);
- grids and layout;
- colour in design;
- logos and marks;
- brand systems;
- posters and editorial;
- UI and screens (it can use KurenaiOS's own design system as a case
  study);
- print and export (resolution, colour spaces, bleed).

**Projects:**
- a logo and mini brand for a fictional studio;
- a poster for a Collection title;
- a light-novel or manga cover with title design;
- a redesign of a screen;
- a type specimen.

**Interactive drills (in the app; nothing to install):**
- **kerning:** move letters by eye, then compare with a reference;
- **font pairing:** pick and justify, then see the critique;
- **hierarchy:** reorder a layout's text sizes;
- **palette:** build a scheme that passes a contrast check (the app's own
  APCA/WCAG check).

### 2.3 Editing

"Editing" here means **video** first, with photo as a module (decision B).

**Core path:**
- the cut (continuity, match cuts, J and L cuts);
- pacing and rhythm;
- sound (music sync, beat-mapping, levels);
- story in the edit;
- colour (correction, then grading);
- titles and motion graphics basics;
- export and platforms.

**Projects:**
- an AMV to a song you choose, cut to the beat;
- a trailer for a Collection title;
- a 60-second montage from your own footage.

**In the app:**
- a **beat map** (tap along to a song, keep the markers, export them as a
  text list for the editor);
- **storyboard** pages in the sketchbook (frames with shot and timing
  notes);
- **shot lists**;
- the theory cards.

The editing itself happens in DaVinci Resolve or LumaFusion on the iPad,
or CapCut (decision C).

**Photo module** (later): exposure, white balance, curves, masking,
retouching ethics; projects are an edit series of five photos.

### 2.4 Writing

**Core path:**
- the sentence and the paragraph;
- point of view and voice;
- scene and sequel;
- dialogue (including how VNs and LNs carry it);
- description and the senses;
- character (want, need, flaw);
- structure (three acts, kishōtenketsu, the light-novel volume);
- revision (cutting, line editing, reading aloud);
- forms: flash fiction, the short story, poetry, the script.

**Projects:**
- a flash fiction a week for a month;
- a short story through three drafts;
- a VN route outline with a branching scene (the branches in a small
  script format the app can preview);
- a light-novel chapter.

**In the app:**
- a **writing desk:** a distraction-free Markdown editor with word count,
  versions, and a diff between drafts that shows revision;
- **sprints** run in Focus (a word target and a timer);
- **reading as a writer** from the Collection: pick a book or VN you
  finished, and answer the lesson's craft questions about it.

## 3. A lesson

Lessons use the shared topic page, like Code's (invariant 104's notes
contract, adapted):

1. **Notes:** short pages (the notes layout rule: lines and bullets, with
   annotated `{fig}` diagrams wherever a picture explains it). Every visual
   lesson has worked demonstrations as step images, for example "a head in
   six steps".
2. **The exercise:** what to make, how long it takes, and its rubric.
   Visual exercises open in the sketchbook (iPad) or say which app to use;
   writing opens the desk.
3. **Self-critique:** the rubric's rows, ticked with a sentence each. The
   rubric is the lesson's ("Are the boxes' vanishing points consistent?"),
   never a generic "good/bad".
4. **Theory cards** (FSRS): terms and principles, for example "What does
   a J cut do?" or "Name the three properties of a colour".
5. **Optional: ask Kurenai** for a critique (decision E).

**Status from evidence**, as in Code (invariant 107):
- notes read;
- the exercise saved;
- the self-critique done;
- a theory card proven.

The status follows these four by itself, and reading alone never completes
a lesson.

## 4. Evidence and the portfolio

- **The portfolio** holds every saved exercise, study and piece:
  - the image, a time-lapse video, a text, a link or a PDF;
  - its lesson or project, the date, the time spent (from Focus when the
    session was linked), the tools, the reference used, the self-critique,
    and Kurenai's critique if one was asked for.
- **Storage:** a new IndexedDB, `kurenai-os-creatives`, for the files.
  The record (titles, dates, critique text, links) is small and syncs. The
  files upload only when you say so, like topic attachments (invariant 36,
  decision F).
- **Bringing work in from another app:**
  - share or save from Procreate (a PNG and the time-lapse MP4), Clip
    Studio, Affinity or Resolve;
  - then "Add to portfolio" in the app, or the share sheet once it is
    installed (a Web Share Target is supported on iPadOS for Home Screen
    apps; checked in the spike).
- **Hours** come from Focus. A Creatives session links a track and a
  lesson or project, exactly like a study session, and a switch mid-way
  works (invariant 4a).

## 5. Practice and scheduling

### 5.1 The day

A **Daily Creative** (like Code's Daily 20) is computed from the evidence:
- one drill (for example 10 minutes of gesture);
- one lesson step, or the next milestone of a piece;
- the due theory cards.

It is never stored, and it is the same all day.

### 5.2 Theory on FSRS

Theory cards are ordinary FSRS cards (invariant 111) with keys
`creatives:<lesson>:<i>`, through `KOS.srs.weights()`. Nothing new.

### 5.3 The redo

An exercise marked "redo" comes back on an FSRS interval, as weeks and
months, not days. Its review is doing the exercise again, with the old
attempt hidden until you finish. Then the two are shown side by side and
you rate the redo. It is the same idea as an exam's redo items
(invariant 124).

### 5.4 References

- Gesture and study drills need reference images. These are **yours
  only** (decision G): a folder you import, photos you take, or a frame
  from your own media.
- Nothing is hot-linked from a stock site and nothing is bundled, so no
  licence questions arise.
- The Collection's covers can be colour-study references for private
  practice, and are never exported with a piece.

## 6. Rewards (the Governor)

These follow the existing invariants:
- **Creatives is study.** A linked Focus session pays by the Focus rules
  (invariant 4a). It is not leisure, so it is not the rest streak
  (invariant 3).
- **Proven evidence pays once**, as Code's tasks do (invariant 106):
  - an exercise with its self-critique: 12 XP and 1 gold;
  - a theory card proven: +4 XP, within the shared daily cap;
  - a piece submitted with every milestone and its rubric: like a
    capstone (150 XP, 40 gold, +10 HP).
  - Re-saving, redoing or asking for a critique pays nothing more.
- **Writing words, strokes or hours pays nothing by itself**, so volume is
  never farmed.

## 7. Kurenai's role (decision E)

- **A critique on request:** the piece (an image, an excerpt or a link's
  description), the brief and the rubric, sent in one request after a
  "What is sent" dialog (invariant 102b). The reply speaks to the rubric's
  rows: what works, and the one thing to fix next.
- **Never:** generating images, rewriting your prose, or "finishing"
  anything. The Assistant has read tools (`creatives_overview`,
  `creatives_portfolio`) and no write tool for evidence, as with Code
  (invariant 110).

## 8. Joins with the rest of the app

- **Goals:** types for pieces finished, drill days, words written and
  Creatives hours (invariant 5b), measured by reading the portfolio and
  Focus.
- **Reports:** `f.creatives` holds lessons done, pieces, drills, words and
  hours, as totals only (invariant 102b). The Collection card can show
  "made this month" thumbnails only when you turn it on.
- **The Collection:** "made from" links a piece to the title it is fan
  art of, an AMV to its anime, a story to the VN that inspired it.
- **Japanese:** the VN sprite and manga projects can make art for the
  Japanese cast (its plan §12.1) instead of using Gemini.
- **Calendar and Pacing:** a project's milestones can be dated, and drawn
  as derived chips (invariant 44).

## 9. The iPad and the sketchbook

This depends on the iPad plan's ink engine (6.1). The sketchbook adds:
- **Layers-lite:** a sketch layer at 30%, ink and colour. Three layers,
  opacity, hide, and merge down.
- **A few brushes:** pencil, ink (tapering), marker, flat colour and
  eraser. Width comes from speed and tilt (the Metapen) or pressure (the
  Pencil Pro).
- **Drill tools:** a timer that clears the page between gestures, a
  reference panel, a mirror view (flip to check proportions), and
  greyscale preview for value.
- **Export** to PNG, or to Procreate as an image to finish.

It stays a drills-and-studies tool. Anything that wants 50 layers belongs
in Procreate.

## 12. Questions for you (they set the starting points)

1. **Where are you now in each track?** Have you drawn before
   (traditional, digital, how often), designed anything, edited video, or
   written fiction?
2. **What is it for?** A hobby beside A-levels, a portfolio for a
   university application (Art or Design courses ask for one), content you
   want to post, or making your own VN or manga?
3. **Which apps do you have or want on the iPad?** Procreate (one-off
   purchase), Clip Studio, Affinity, DaVinci Resolve (free on iPad), CapCut
   or LumaFusion.
4. **"Editing":** video, photo, or both?
5. **Time:** how much a day or week, against Code, Japanese and the
   exams?
6. **Order:** which track first? Art is proposed because it uses the iPad
   most; Writing can start without the ink engine.

## 14. Invariants this would add (draft, numbered after the iPad's)

- **134. CREATIVE EVIDENCE IS MADE, NOT CLAIMED.**
  - A lesson's checks come from saved work, its self-critique and proven
    theory cards, written only by the Creatives core.
  - Each proof pays once.
  - Volume (words, strokes, minutes) pays nothing beyond Focus.
- **135. KURENAI CRITIQUES, NEVER CREATES.**
  - No tool generates or rewrites a piece.
  - A critique sends only what its "What is sent" dialog shows, once per
    press.
- **136. THE PORTFOLIO IS LOCAL FIRST.**
  - Files live in `kurenai-os-creatives` and upload only on request.
  - The record syncs.
  - References are the user's own, and nothing is bundled.

## 15. Risks

- **Too wide.** Four disciplines can each fill years. Release one track at
  a time, and keep the core path short: fundamentals only, then projects.
- **Self-critique turns into ticking boxes.** Each row needs a sentence,
  and the redo makes the honest comparison for you.
- **Time against the exams.** Creatives is study in the Governor's eyes,
  but Exam Season (invariant 123) should be able to hold its Daily
  Creative. That is a switch on the desk, on by default during the season.
